\section{What We Are Proposing}

\subsection{The System}

The Legal RAG System is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation document review platform
built specifically for Pakistani legal due diligence. It is not an off-the-shelf
international product --- it is configured for Pakistani law, Pakistani document
formats, and Pakistani regulatory requirements.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Accepts your PDF document bundle (property deeds, NOCs, CNICs, tax receipts)
  \item Runs 15+ due diligence questions automatically
  \item Cross-references every finding against Pakistani statutes and the Constitution
  \item Generates a branded review memorandum with your firm's letterhead
  \item Allows your lawyer to ask additional questions in natural language
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What Makes It Specific to Pakistan}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Pakistani statutory corpus:} Constitution of Pakistan 1973,
    Transfer of Property Act 1882, Registration Act 1908, Stamp Act 1899,
    Companies Act 2017, Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Benami Transactions
    (Prohibition) Act 2017, Anti-Money Laundering Act 2010
  \item \textbf{Urdu document support:} Handles mixed Urdu-English documents,
    Fard-e-Malkiat, Intiqal, and Khasra references natively
  \item \textbf{City-specific NOC requirements:} Separate profiles for CDA
    (Islamabad), RDA (Rawalpindi), LDA (Lahore), SBCA (Karachi)
  \item \textbf{FBR compliance:} Automatic Section 236C/236K withholding tax
    checks for transactions above PKR 5 million
  \item \textbf{Inheritance detection:} Automatically triggers succession
    compliance checklist when inherited property is detected
\end{itemize}
